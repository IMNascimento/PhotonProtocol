A camera whose shutter opens while this screen is changing records half of one code and half of the next, and half a code is worth nothing. Holding each one for longer is what prevents that. Two is the fastest the format allows and only works when the camera is fast and well matched; four is a safe default for a phone filming a monitor. Raise it further if the receiving page reports pictures caught mid-change.

Getting a good recording

  • Screen brightness to maximum; turn off auto-brightness and any night or warmth filter.
  • Fill as much of the camera frame with this screen as you can.
  • Record at the highest resolution the camera offers.
  • Avoid glare — a reflection across the codes costs frames.
  • Keep recording past one full pass. Extra frames carry repair data and are never wasted.
