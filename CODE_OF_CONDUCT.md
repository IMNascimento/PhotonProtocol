# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want PhotonProtocol to be a project people are glad to work on. Everyone
taking part — contributors, reviewers, maintainers, people opening their first
issue — is entitled to participate free of harassment, regardless of experience,
background, identity, or how well they write English.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, especially when you disagree.
- Critique the work, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person is trying to help until you have real evidence
  otherwise.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and that this is not a
  judgement of you.
- Remember that many contributors are not writing in their first language.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Dismissing someone on the basis of who they are rather than what they wrote.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code comments — and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers through a private channel: a direct message
on GitHub, or a security advisory if the issue must not be public. Reports are
handled confidentially, and the person reporting is never expected to confront
the person they are reporting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may
temporarily or permanently block anyone from participating. Enforcement aims to
be proportionate: a first lapse usually earns a private word, not a ban.
